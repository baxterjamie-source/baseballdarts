👥 Play Mode
Darts Baseball
Two players vs one? Sure!
Lone Wolves is the classic game — every player scores for themselves.
Team lets players gang up — up to 4 teams, any size, scores shared. A beginner and their friend can team up against a single expert, or you can just play 2‑vs‑2.
If Equalizer Mode is on at the same time, every bonus applies to the team, not the individual — trailing teams get more chances at bonus runs since every teammate's darts count toward the same target.
Play Mode locks once a game starts — start a new game to switch between Lone Wolves and Team.
